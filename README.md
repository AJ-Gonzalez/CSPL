# Career Showcase and Portfolio License (CSPL)

**Version 1.0**

A software license designed for career-focused developers who want to showcase their skills without signaling entrepreneurial pursuits or raising red flags with enterprise employers.

## The Problem

There's a growing sentiment in enterprise sectors that extensive open source contributions or personal projects can be viewed as red flags during hiring:

- **Time commitment concerns**: Some employers worry that developers heavily invested in open source may prioritize personal projects over their primary role
- **Intellectual property risks**: Companies fear potential IP contamination or conflicts of interest from developers maintaining competing open source projects
- **Entrepreneurial signals**: Active open source maintenance can signal future startup ambitions rather than career commitment
- **Corporate culture fit**: Some enterprises prefer developers focused solely on proprietary commercial software

Meanwhile, developers still need portfolios to demonstrate their skills and advance their careers.

## The Solution

The **Career Showcase and Portfolio License (CSPL)** addresses this tension by:

**Explicitly signaling career focus** - Built-in language that communicates commitment to primary employment  
**Allowing portfolio visibility** - Employers and recruiters can freely view and evaluate your code  
**Permitting closed-source commercial use** - Shows you understand business value and aren't anti-commercial  
**Blocking open source distribution** - Prevents your code from becoming public open source projects  
**Preventing competitive concerns** - Can't be used to build competing open source alternatives

## Who Should Use CSPL

This license is ideal for developers who:

- Work in enterprise environments where open source is viewed skeptically
- Want to demonstrate skills without appearing to have "side hustle" ambitions
- Need a portfolio for career advancement without triggering employer concerns
- Understand commercial software and want to show pragmatic business alignment
- Spend the majority of their development time in their primary role

## License Features

### Permissions
- View and evaluate (for hiring/assessment)
- Use in closed-source commercial products
- Modify for internal/proprietary use
- Distribute in compiled/binary form as part of larger applications

### Restrictions
- No open source distribution or incorporation
- No standalone redistribution as libraries or products
- No rebranding as original work
- Must maintain attribution

### Protections
- Standard "AS IS" warranty disclaimer
- No liability for the author
- Clear termination clause for violations

## How to Apply CSPL to Your Project

### Step 1: Add the License File

Download `CSPL-LICENSE.txt` from this repository and add it to your project as `LICENSE` or `LICENSE.txt`.

Fill in the copyright information:
```
Copyright (c) [YEAR] [YOUR NAME]
```

### Step 2: Update Your README

Add a clear license notice to your project's README.md:

```markdown
## License

This project is licensed under the Career Showcase and Portfolio License (CSPL) v1.0  
Copyright (c) 2025 Your Name

**TL;DR:**
- View and evaluate for hiring purposes
- Use in closed-source commercial products (with attribution)
- No open source distribution
- No standalone redistribution

See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for full terms.
```

### Step 3: Add to Repository Metadata

On GitHub, you can add "CSPL-1.0" as a custom license identifier in your repository settings.

## FAQ

### Q: Why not just use MIT or Apache?
**A:** Traditional open source licenses signal openness to community contributions and maintenance, which some enterprise employers view as a potential time commitment risk or IP concern. CSPL explicitly signals career focus.

### Q: Can companies still hire me and use my code?
**A:** Absolutely! Companies can evaluate your code during interviews and even use it in their closed-source products. They just can't open source it or redistribute it independently.

### Q: Isn't this against the spirit of open source?
**A:** CSPL isn't meant to replace open source - it's an alternative for developers who face career obstacles from open source contributions in certain corporate environments. It's about giving developers options based on their career context.

### Q: What if someone violates the license?
**A:** The license includes termination clauses. Violations immediately terminate all rights, and the violator must cease use and destroy copies. However, this is primarily a portfolio license - enforcement is at your discretion.

### Q: Can I use CSPL for commercial projects?
**A:** CSPL is designed for portfolio/showcase projects. For commercial products, you'd typically use proprietary licensing or a commercial-friendly open source license depending on your business model.

### Q: Does this prevent me from contributing to other open source projects?
**A:** Not at all! CSPL only applies to projects you explicitly license under it. You can still contribute to other open source projects - CSPL just gives you an option for career-focused portfolio pieces.
